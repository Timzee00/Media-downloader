Frequently asked questions

Which sites are supported?

Media Downloader uses media extraction tools that work with many popular social and video platforms. Actual support changes over time and can vary per video, region and platform restriction.

How long are downloads saved?

Downloaded files are held temporarily on the service. Retention settings depend on hosting and storage availability, and a restart or deployment on temporary hosting can remove them sooner. Save important files directly to your device.

Why does an old History item say Save failed?

The file may have expired or been removed from temporary server storage. If the original link is still known and the source is accessible, it may be possible to download it again. Old records without their original links cannot always be recovered.

Can I save private or protected media?

Private, paywalled, deleted or DRM-protected content may be unavailable. You must have appropriate rights or permission to download media.

Is download history permanent?

No. Some history is stored in your browser and some on the server. Clearing browser data, changing devices or losing temporary server storage can affect your history. Treat history as a convenience, not a permanent backup.
