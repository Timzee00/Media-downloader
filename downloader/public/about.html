Use Clipora responsibly

Only download material you own, are licensed to use, or have permission to save. Respect creators' rights, platform rules and applicable laws. Clipora does not claim to bypass paid access or digital-rights protections. Some media may not be retrievable if a source removes or restricts it.

Links and profile details you publish through Link Tree Studio may be visible to anyone who has your public page URL. Do not publish passwords, private contact details or other information you do not intend to share.

Frequently asked questions

What is Clipora?

Clipora is a Timzee Corp platform with two tools: Media Downloader for supported media links and Link Tree Studio for creating a public page that gathers your important links.

Which media sites are supported?

Media Downloader uses extraction tools that work with many popular social and video platforms. Actual support changes over time and can vary by link, region and platform restrictions.

Are my Link Tree pages public?

Your published page is designed to be shared through its public URL. Only add profile details and destinations you are comfortable making available to people who can access that link.

How long are downloads saved?

Downloaded files are held temporarily on the service. Retention depends on hosting and storage availability, and a restart or deployment on temporary hosting can remove them sooner. Save important files directly to your device.

Why might a download fail?

A source may be unavailable, restrict access, change its delivery method or provide media that the downloader cannot currently process. Try again later or check whether the original link is still accessible.

Who operates Clipora?

Clipora is a Timzee Corp platform. The available tools and supported sources may change as the service is maintained.
